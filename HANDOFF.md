- Smart PDF facts learned (all in smart.rs doc comments): sheets are bookmarks titled `X.SchDoc(Name)`; a bookmark with Components/Nets but no `.SchDoc` (Top Assembly, Multilayer Composite Print) is a PCB print, ignored with a note. Components list units (`U1A`) with pins `U1-C5`. **Net names are per sheet** (Altium's default: ports do not name nets), so one net can be `NetR87_1` on one sheet and `NetSB45_2` on another; the net-level Ports bookmark is titled with the net name and its `_FindWord(n, "POSAI40CK1")` carries the port's own name; the sheet-level Ports section maps that word to the real name. Hidden search words spell every non-alphanumeric char as `0` (`SD_CS` → `NLSD0CS`, pin `U1-G9` → `PIU10G9`). Join rule (smart.rs `join_nets`): flat design → names and port words global; hierarchy → nets join by name only when no label names them (power ports), and by port word among siblings and to the parent's net of that name; a note says same-named sheet entries are taken as wired. Property popups (`ShowCompProps_<guid>()` → `app.popUpMenu("Key: Value", ...)`) are bound one-to-one per page by CO/PI words inside the link rect, best score first (strict-inside words, then slack 1.5 pt, then distance). llPDFLib PDFs (some ST) put all search words at one point: no binding possible, note says so (stage 2: bind by drawn designator text). Xyloni is an Acrobat binder with a T8F81 page first: popups on pages no sheet leads to are ignored.
